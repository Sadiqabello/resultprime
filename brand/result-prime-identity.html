__SPARKMINI__ Voice & tone

Plain words. Real numbers. No surprises.

We speak to head teachers, exam officers and parents — busy people who need to trust what they read. Clear, warm, and grounded; never jargon, never hype.

__SPARK20__ We sound like

  • “Your results are published. Parents can view them now.”
  • “Every change is recorded — who, and when.”
  • “Set up your school in a day. We’ll walk you through it.”
  • Confident, specific, encouraging.

We avoid

  • “Revolutionary AI-powered synergy platform.”
  • “Leverage best-in-class result paradigms.”
  • Shouting, exclamation marks, false urgency.
  • Acronyms a parent wouldn’t know.
